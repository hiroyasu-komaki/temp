PART III　MEASUREMENT

Drawing the current line — three layers, three levels, A1–A5, evidence

Change the question from "can you?" to "did you?" Capability has an answer only inside the person; behaviour leaves an external form — records and artefacts.
Measurement is not the objective. It fixes the starting point for Strategy B.

11The three-layer structure

Conventional skill management expresses capability in a single taxonomy, and we ran that model for five years. It breaks down for one reason — the capability our staff have actually been exercising does not exist in the standard taxonomy.

What must not be overlooked
The items the standard taxonomy "cannot measure" are not worthless because they cannot be measured.
The reason the business ran for decades, uninterrupted, while using external parties, is that these capabilities existed. They are therefore quite likely to be our substantive competitive strength.

Because the taxonomy runs on two tracks, the scale must too. The base layer uses the vendor's five levels; overlay A uses three levels. Forcing them into a single scale means distorting one of them for the sake of unification.

13The three levels

A one-dimensional scale of beginner / intermediate / advanced cannot separate the error that dominates here. Unless you provide a scale that can separate, you will not get a separated answer.

LevelDefinition, in terms of behaviour
Can executeIn this domain, there is a record of designing, building or operating to completion with one's own hands
Can evaluateNo record of execution, but a record of sending back, requesting changes to, or proposing alternatives to an external deliverable or estimate, with reasons
Custodial onlyCarries the responsibility, but neither of the above records exists
The middle level is the core
"Can evaluate" is the core capability under long-term external dependence.
To keep buying from outside, someone must be able to judge whether what was bought is sound. Without that, it is delegation, not procurement. This level is the operational definition of absorptive capacity (→ Part I, Ch. 5).

And what must be detected is the third level. Where "custodial only" is distributed shows where the organisation stops. The purpose of the inventory is not to collect good values.

"Proposing an alternative" is the strongest single indicator. Sending something back or requesting a change can happen because someone was asked to; proposing an alternative is action taken without being asked, and does not occur unless the external proposal has been independently evaluated.
14Changing the question — from "can you?" to "did you?"
The conventional questionThe question here
"What is your skill level in this domain?""In the last three years, what did you do?"
Asks about capability. The only answer lies inside the personAsks about behaviour. Records and artefacts remain as external form

Even as self-report, a report of behaviour is far more reliable than a report of capability. "I am advanced" cannot be verified; "I sent this estimate back" can.

This is the same form as an established idea in performance-appraisal research (behaviourally anchored rating scales, BARS): making raters refer to concrete behavioural instances rather than abstract merit reduces variance in how the scale is interpreted.

The way the question is framed determines the form of the intervention.

What measurement asks aboutWhat the intervention changes
Capability (can you?)→ the person (train them)
Behaviour (did you?)→ the work (make it happen)

We take the latter. The consequence is developed in Part VI, Ch. 33.

15A1–A5 — the five intermediation activities

Overlay A (vendor control) is broken into five behaviours. These determine what kinds of trace are inspected.

ItemBehaviour in questionTrace inspected
A1 Requirement translationDid the employee put the business unit's request into specification language?Author field of the requirements document, RFP, specification

A4 is the strongest evidence of the five. A1–A3 can occur simply because someone was asked; proposing an alternative is action taken unprompted. It therefore does not occur unless the process demands it — and conversely, it does occur when the process demands it.

16Three types of error, and three grades of evidence

The reason each layer is measured differently is that the dominant type of error differs by layer.

TypeContentCancelled by aggregation?Corrected by peer visibility?
① Random errorIndividual variation (the meticulous under-rate, the bold over-rate)Yes—
② Motivated errorDeliberate exaggeration, conscious of effects on rating or payNoYes
③ Structural errorThe whole organisation is mistaken within the same frame. No bad faith, no falsehoodNoNo

Peer visibility works powerfully against ②, because colleagues can see exaggeration. Against ③ it is powerless, because colleagues share the same frame.

"Someone who has owned that system for fifteen years knows it well" — this is a belief the entire organisation shares, and nobody disputes it. Yet whether that "knowing" means "can implement" or "is responsible for" is indistinguishable to the person and to their colleagues alike.

What our five years failed to reach was precisely ③. The accurate statement is therefore not "self-assessment is inadequate" but "self-assessment is powerless against ③, and ③ was our misdiagnosis".

This is not a weakness of any particular product. Even a standards-compliant taxonomy will pass type ③ straight through, so long as proficiency is self-reported on a one-dimensional scale. Product selection does not fix what overlay A misses. What fixes it is the measurement protocol.

Type ③ concentrates in overlays A and B. In the base layer the dominant errors are ① and ②, which aggregation and peer visibility do correct. The base layer can therefore run on the vendor's five levels with self-reporting. Reinforcement is needed only for the overlays.

Three grades of evidence

Self-reporting is demoted from a determining instrument to supporting information.

GradeContent
Primary (records)Production access rights, who produced the effort estimate in an approval request, change and acceptance records — verifiable without asking the person
Secondary (artefacts)Design documents and comparison studies written by the person; reconciliation notes — cannot be manufactured after the fact
Tertiary (self-report)Does not determine a level on its own. Supporting use only, where primary and secondary evidence are absent
17The "unverified" flag, and how to read the output

Where no primary or secondary evidence exists and no determination can be made, the entry is recorded as "unverified", not as "low level". On the inventory it appears as a blank.

Rule for reading the output
In the first round of measurement, large numbers of "custodial only" and "unverified" entries are the normal result.
Their appearance is the evidence that measurement worked. The criterion is not that the values are good but that they are correct. Set "better inventory values" as the goal and measurement repeats the failure of five years ago.

Guarding against misreading (addressing C11)

Presented raw, the output will be misread. The forms of misreading can be identified in advance, so they are handled on the presentation side.

Predictable misreadingCountermeasure in presentation
Reading a stage difference as a capability differenceDo not place sites side by side. State explicitly in the output that the scope measured differs by site
Reading it as a poor rating of veteransState alongside that "custodial only" is not an assessment of the individual but an indication of how far the organisation can run on its own in that domain. A veteran of decades scoring low is a failure of measurement, not an assessment of that person
Reading it as a cost-cutting programmeRestate the reason for leading with overlay A as a matter of perception, not control (below)
Reading it as connected to compensationPut the disconnection into a written rule or notice (→ Part V, Ch. 29)
Why overlay A (R4) comes first — restated
Measuring R4 first is not about control. It is about restoring perception.
When R4 improves, estimates tighten, more work is sent back, and unnecessary features drop out — the financial effect is direct, measurable and fast. Improvements in execution capability, by contrast, are diffuse and slow. The asymmetry is unavoidable, so regardless of intent, leading with R4 is liable to be read as cost-cutting.
But when R4 is "custodial only", the organisation cannot judge whether an external deliverable is sound. Before it is a spending problem, this is a problem of an organisation unable to perceive its own state. For five years R4 was not a measured item and was never once assessed — this is exactly where perception was missing.
18Calibration through anchor cases

Unifying vocabulary and criteria is not enough to align judgements across sites, because the interpretation of criterion statements drifts site by site.

So instead of abstract criterion statements, real projects and artefacts are designated as "this is the evaluation level" and shared across all sites. When a judgement is uncertain, compare against the anchor, not the wording.

ItemRule
Authority to select and reviseThe function line head (→ Part V, Ch. 28)
Revision historyMandatory. Without it the site heads will not accept the judgement. It is the only check on arbitrary calibration
Handling cross-site divergenceDetect it and record how it was resolved. The presence of that record is what determines the line head's own level
Anchors can also be generated by having sites actually take over each other's work. The exchange leaves records — change records, artefacts, review records — which are themselves primary and secondary evidence, so this is not extra work for calibration but part of running the measurement (→ Part V, Ch. 31).

R4 cannot remain the only scope

A2 (judging the reasonableness of effort) and A4 (proposing alternatives) demand technical judgement. You cannot produce a design alternative without knowing the design; you cannot judge an estimate without knowing the work.

"Can evaluate" is not an independent capability. It rests on residual execution knowledge. Neglect the base layer while investing only in R4, and R4 will fall too, with a lag. There is no steady state in which R4 alone is sustained.
