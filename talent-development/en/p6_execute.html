PART VI　EXECUTION

Making accumulation happen — form revisions, reconciliation, enabling conditions

The response to what the second figure calls "maintaining organisational discipline during the flat phase".
Our answer to that difficulty is not motivation or awareness-raising, but revision of business forms.

33Position — capability is raised by changing the work, not the person

Since measurement asks about behaviour ("did you?"), the intervention necessarily changes the work.

The unit of intervention is not the individual. It is business processes and forms.

There is a second reason for this position: a self-referential problem.

The framework assumes that the absorptive capacity for external knowledge is governed by the internal knowledge already held. Applied straightforwardly, that assumption yields the following.

An unavoidable consequence
An organisation in the "custodial only" state cannot design, by itself, the plan for getting out of it.
If a manager told to write a development plan is themselves custodial-only in that very domain, on what basis do they write it? They were classified as custodial-only precisely because they cannot judge technical soundness; asking them to design "how to develop this" contradicts the finding.

Interventions take the form of "change the form" rather than "write a plan" as a response to that consequence. A manager who cannot judge technical soundness can still add one field to an approval form. Revising a process does not depend on capability level.

Where training sits — not ineffective, but out of sequence

This is not a rejection of training programmes. Within this framework, however, they are supporting, for the simple reason that training leaves no traces. An attendance record is not evidence of having independently verified an external deliverable.

Scope
Designing training content (what to teach)Out of scope
The procedure that determines who is trained (who is missing what)In scope. Reconciliation produces this
Training delivered before reconciliation has identified "what could not be understood" is investment aimed at the wrong target. Training is not ineffective; it is a question of sequence.
34Building A1–A5 into business processes

These five are the core of the intervention. All are process changes; almost no cost is incurred. What is incurred is effort (→ Ch. 36).

A1　Requirement translation

Current (assumed)The vendor drafts the requirements document and RFP; employees review and approve
RevisionFix the author of the first draft as an employee. The vendor moves to the reviewing side
Trace producedAuthor field of the requirements document, RFP and specification
ResistanceLow. First-draft quality falls, but review recovers it

A2　Verification of effort estimates

Current (assumed)The vendor's quote is transcribed into the approval request as-is
RevisionAdd an "in-house estimate" field to the approval form. The employee estimates before the vendor's quote is presented, and records the difference between the two and the reason for it. The request cannot be circulated with the field blank
Trace producedWho produced the effort estimate in the approval request / budget
ResistanceMedium. Expect the objection "the problem is that we can't estimate"
Accuracy is not the point. Being wrong is fine. Writing down why the estimate was wrong is what builds capability; being right is not the objective. Mistake this and make accuracy a rated criterion, and employees will ask the vendor for the number before filling in the field — the form survives and the behaviour disappears.

A3　Acceptance judgement

Current (assumed)An acceptance stamp only. No record of rejections exists
RevisionAdd a "rejection deliberation" field to the acceptance record. Even where nothing is sent back, record what was checked and why the decision was not to send it back
Trace producedAcceptance record / record of rejections and change requests
ResistanceMedium. Acceptance shifts from a formality to an act of judgement, so it takes longer

A4　Proposing alternatives

Current (assumed)Only the vendor's proposal is retained
RevisionAbove a set value, a case cannot go forward for approval without one option other than the vendor's proposal attached. "Continue as-is" and "do not proceed" count as options
Trace producedDesign-approach deliberation records, comparison documents
ResistanceHigh. It changes the approval requirement itself, affecting decision speed
A4 is the strongest evidence of the five and simultaneously the most resisted. Proposing an alternative is action taken unprompted, so it does not occur unless the process demands it. Conversely, it does occur when the process demands it.

A5　Incident triage

Current (assumed)The first-line triage field in incident records is filled with vendor names
RevisionMake the first-line triage owner field mandatory, and require the employee to record an initial triage hypothesis before escalating to the vendor
Trace producedWho performed first-line triage in the incident record
ResistanceMedium. One extra step during an incident raises concern about slower initial response
The hypothesis may be wrong. A record of a wrong hypothesis is exactly as much evidence that judgement took place as a record of a right one.

Side effect — C3 is solved at the same time

All five are form revisions. Executing them therefore builds the record-keeping regime automatically.

The intervention against degraded measurement is simultaneously the intervention against "traces are not recorded" (C3). Add fields to the forms and next year's inspection target is those fields. Not parallel work — the same work.
The "current (assumed)" descriptions above are assumptions that have not been verified. The actual forms and practices need to be confirmed before the revisions are specified in detail.
35Reconciliation — without it, execution is nominal

Every intervention above stops at recording a prediction: the in-house estimate, the reason for not sending something back, the initial triage hypothesis. All are records whose outcome becomes known later.

All five are built as pairs, yet nowhere is there a step that puts the two halves together. That is what nominal execution actually is. Fill in the estimate field without ever comparing it against actuals, and next year the estimate will be wrong by the same margin.

The remedy — add one reconciliation field to each form

The character of the intervention does not change. As in the previous chapter, it is a form revision.

ItemField addedWhen it is completed
A1Number of rework items, and which part of the requirement caused themAt acceptance
A2Variance against actual effort, and the reason for itAt project close
A3Defects that occurred after go-live, and what should have been checked at acceptance3 months after go-live
A4Whether the alternative was adopted, and the criterion that decided itAt approval
A5Confirmed root cause, and how it differed from the initial hypothesisAt restoration
What is recorded is not "were you right?" but "why were you wrong?" Record only the verdict and it becomes an assessment of accuracy. The caution under A2 — make accuracy a rated criterion and employees will ask the vendor for the number first — applies here unchanged.

Reconciliation notes are evidence for determining level

Whether someone can break down "why it was wrong" is strong evidence of level. Those who can decompose the reason are evaluating that domain; those who cannot have merely filled in a field. Because such notes cannot be manufactured after the fact, they count as secondary evidence.

Sequence — "transfer first, then execute" does not work

Who inside the company holds the know-how that would be transferred? How to read effort, what to look for at acceptance — a substantial part of it sits on the vendor side. That is what being "custodial only" means. Make transfer a precondition of execution and there is no source to transfer from — structurally identical to the self-referential problem in Ch. 33.

What accumulation actually is
Knowledge need not precede action. It must, however, follow immediately after it. And the accumulation of reconciliation notes becomes the first transferable material.
Under this design, "asking the vendor why the estimate was wrong" is a legitimate act of learning. It is the shortest path to A2 reconciliation, and once recorded it becomes material for the next person in the role. Rather than procuring transferable material from outside, reconciliation generates it.
The knowledge spiral (SECI) turns not in a training room but inside the field where prediction and outcome are compared.
Frequency — full coverage is impossible. Reconciliation carries additional effort and cannot be done on every case. Confine it to the cases selected under enabling condition ① (allocation of opportunity). The cases chosen as A2 and A4 targets become the reconciliation targets — selection is done once.
36Enabling conditions — without these, nothing happens

The process changes in Ch. 34 do not operate on their own. Four conditions are prerequisite (C7).

Condition ① Allocation of opportunity
the opportunity to evaluate is scarce, and most of it currently sits with the vendor
What must be decidedWhat happens if it is not
How many of the year's cases are A2/A4 targetsTarget everything and the work does not run; set no number and not one case is done
To whom each case is assignedIt goes to whoever has capacity, which does not match the development target
Condition ② Tolerance of failure
A3 (rejection) and A4 (proposing alternatives) are acts that can be wrong

Something is sent back, turns out to have been fine, and the schedule slips two weeks. How is that person then treated? In an organisation where this is undefined, traces never appear, because not sending anything back is always the safe and rational choice.

The act of sending work back or proposing an alternative does not, in itself, lower anyone's rating — regardless of whether the judgement turns out to be right.

Unless that is put in writing, condition ② is not met. A verbal assurance disappears at the next reorganisation.

Condition ③ Time
A2 (producing an independent estimate) is pure additional effort
What must be decidedWhat happens if it is not
How many hours per case are allowed for A2/A4Instruct without allowing for it and you get either overtime or a formality
Whose working time it comes fromWithout a source, the individual absorbs it
Change the forms without securing the time and the organisation starts producing work whose purpose is to produce traces. It is not falsification — people ask the vendor for a number in order to fill the field. Traces without behaviour appear, and measurement drifts from reality again. The same shape of failure as five years ago.
Condition ④ Motive to transfer (specific to design-rationale knowledge)
it requires the willingness of the person transferring

For an employee who has supported a domain for twenty years, transferring design-rationale knowledge is also an act that increases their own replaceability. On this single point, the organisation's interest and the individual's interest run in opposite directions.

Unless there is an assurance that transferring will not disadvantage the person, transfer of design-rationale knowledge will not take place.

This is not a problem with the intervention but a precondition of it. Compensation systems are out of scope for this framework, but the item cannot be put into a plan while it is left out of scope.

Diagnosing the enabling conditions

Before making a plan, check whether these questions can be answered. If they cannot, the intervention will not operate in that domain.

  1. In the past year, how many cases did employees send back at acceptance? If zero — was that because quality was high, or because sending back was not possible?
  2. How was the person who sent something back treated afterwards?
  3. In how many cases did an employee independently re-estimate the vendor's quote?
  4. Whose budget and capacity carries the time allowed for A2 and A4?
  5. For domains where design-rationale knowledge sits with one person, has transfer ever been requested of them? Has it been refused?
37Discipline during the flat phase — what to set as the goal

The difficulty in Strategy B is whether investment and discipline can be sustained until acceleration appears. Set the wrong goal here and the flat phase is not survived.

Policy for year 1

That year's measurement contains a large volume of "unverified" entries, so what should be developed has not been established. Start prematurely and you invest, as five years ago, in targets that do not match reality.
