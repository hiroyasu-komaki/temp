💬 Opinion Formation Simulation in Organizations

First, click "① Scatter opinions randomly" at the lower left. Color represents each person's opinion (blue-ish to red-ish), and everyone starts out a different color. Over the next few seconds to tens of seconds, either everyone converges on the same color (consensus), or several color "islands" remain (opinion splits into factions / echo chambers). Set "Conformity pressure" a bit lower to watch the convergence process unfold slowly.

① Speed of information sharing
The range within which your opinion reaches others, and theirs reaches you. Narrower means someone on "the other island" never hears you.
② Strength of conformity pressure
How much each person moves toward the local average opinion, per tick. Lower means slower convergence, so you can watch the process closely. Zero means a lone wolf unaffected by anyone.
③ Tolerance for differing opinions
Anyone whose opinion is further away than this is ignored as "not worth listening to." Narrower means you only talk to people close to your own opinion (echo chamber formation).
