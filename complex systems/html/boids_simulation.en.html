🐦 Three Simple Rules Are All It Takes to Create a "Flock"

Each particle can only see nearby neighbors, and follows only the three rules below. There is no central conductor. Try switching off the toggles on the right — each rule you remove shows you how the flock falls apart.

Normal individual High local density individual
① Separation (don't collide)
Steer away slightly from neighbors that are too close
② Alignment (match direction)
Match the average heading of nearby neighbors
③ Cohesion (come together)
Drift slowly toward the center of nearby neighbors
